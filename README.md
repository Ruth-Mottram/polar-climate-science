# Polar Regional Climate and Cryosphere Group at DMI

This is the website of our academic research group at The National Centre for Climate Research, a department at the Danish Meteorological Institute

This website is powered by Jekyll and some Bootstrap, Bootwatch. We modified the template of the Allan Lab at University of Munich (previosuly Leiden) - THANKS! Here's a note from the original template providers: 
We tried to make it simple yet adaptable, so that it is easy for you to use it as a template. Plese feel free to copy and modify for your own purposes.  You don't have to link to us or mention us (but of course we appreciate it).

Go to *aboutwebsite.md*  to learn how to copy and modify this page for your purpose.
